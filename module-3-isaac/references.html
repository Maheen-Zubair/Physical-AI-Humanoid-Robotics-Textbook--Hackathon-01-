Recommended Reading Order​

For deeper understanding, we recommend exploring these resources in order:

  1. Start with: Isaac Sim tutorials (hands-on experience)
  2. Then read: Makoviychuk et al. (2021) for Isaac Gym background
  3. For RL: Rudin et al. (2022) on parallel training
  4. For sim-to-real: Tobin et al. (2017) on domain randomization
  5. For deployment: Isaac ROS documentation
