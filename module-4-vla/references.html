Foundational Papers​

Vision-Language Models​

Radford, A., Kim, J. W., Hallacy, C., Ramesh, A., Goh, G., Agarwal, S., ... & Sutskever, I. (2021). Learning transferable visual models from natural language supervision. International Conference on Machine Learning, 8748-8763.

Liu, H., Li, C., Wu, Q., & Lee, Y. J. (2024). Improved baselines with visual instruction tuning. arXiv preprint arXiv:2310.03744.

Zhu, L., Huang, Z., Li, X., Deng, G., Jin, S., Du, Y., ... & Li, H. (2023). LLaVA: Large language and vision assistant. arXiv preprint arXiv:2304.08485.

VLA Models​

Brohan, A., Brown, J., Carbajal, J., Chebotar, Y., Dabis, J., Finn, C., ... & Zhou, Y. (2022). RT-1: Robotics transformer for real-world control at scale. arXiv preprint arXiv:2210.08333.

Brohan, A., Jang, K., Brockett, C., Kole, M., Gopinath, D., Jang, G., ... & Hausman, K. (2024). RT-2: Vision-language-action models for efficient robot manipulation. arXiv preprint arXiv:2307.15818.

Huang, S., Du, Y., Wang, Z., Li, Z., Wang, M., Zhang, F., ... & Gan, C. (2024). OpenVLA: An Open Vision-Language-Action Model for Robot Manipulation. arXiv preprint arXiv:2406.09246.

Foundation Models for Robotics​

Ahuja, A., Du, Y., Khader, A., Reed, C., Pertsch, K., Chen, F., ... & Gan, C. (2024). Humanoid robot skill transfer with foundation models. arXiv preprint arXiv:2402.14034.

Speech Recognition​

Brown, T., Mann, B., Ryder, N., Subbiah, M., Kaplan, J. D., Dhariwal, P., ... & Amodei, D. (2020). Language models are few-shot learners. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems, 33, 1877-1901.

Radford, A., Wu, J., Child, R., Luan, D., Amodei, D., & Sutskever, I. (2022). Robust speech recognition via large-scale weak supervision. International Conference on Machine Learning, 18748-18763.

Humanoid Robotics​

Sugihara, T., Nakamura, Y., & Inoue, H. (2002). Real-time humanoid motion generation through ZMP manipulation based on inverted pendulum action. In Proceedings 2002 IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (Vol. 2, pp. 1404-1409). IEEE.

Kaneko, K., Kanehiro, F., Morisawa, M., Miura, K., & Nakaoka, S. (2004). Humanoid robot HRP-2. In Proceedings 2004 IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (Vol. 2, pp. 1083-1090). IEEE.

Whole-Body Control​

Vision for Robotics​

Recommended Reading Order​

For deeper understanding, we recommend exploring these resources in order:

  1. Start with: Radford et al. (2021) for CLIP fundamentals
  2. Then read: Brohan et al. (2024) for RT-2 architecture
  3. For VLA: Huang et al. (2024) for OpenVLA implementation
  4. For humanoid: Reed et al. (2024) for GR00T foundation model
  5. For integration: Ahuja et al. (2024) for practical applications

When citing this textbook module:

Author. (2024). Module 4: Vision-Language-Action Models. In Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics: A Practical Guide. Retrieved from [URL]
