## Recommended Reading Order

For deeper understanding, we recommend exploring these resources in order:

1. **Start with**: Radford et al. (2021) for CLIP fundamentals
2. **Then read**: Brohan et al. (2024) for RT-2 architecture
3. **For VLA**: Huang et al. (2024) for OpenVLA implementation
4. **For humanoid**: Reed et al. (2024) for GR00T foundation model
5. **For integration**: Ahuja et al. (2024) for practical applications
