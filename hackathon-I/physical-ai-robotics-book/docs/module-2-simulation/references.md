## Recommended Reading Order

For deeper understanding, we recommend exploring these resources in order:

1. **Start with**: Gazebo documentation tutorials (hands-on practice)
2. **Then read**: Koenig & Howard (2004) for historical context
3. **For sim-to-real**: Tobin et al. (2017) on domain randomization
4. **For advanced physics**: Erez et al. (2015) comparing physics engines
