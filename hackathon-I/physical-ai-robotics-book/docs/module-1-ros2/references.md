## Recommended Reading Order

For deeper understanding, we recommend exploring these resources in order:

1. **Start with**: ROS 2 Documentation tutorials (hands-on practice)
2. **Then read**: Macenski et al. (2022) for architectural understanding
3. **For DDS details**: Pardo-Castellote (2003) and eProsima documentation
4. **For advanced topics**: Koubaa (2020) edited volume
